## 3. Gaussian process surrogate

Inputs d/W in [0.05, 0.4] and a/b in [0.5, 2.0]. 60 training solves on a Latin hypercube, 40 independent random test solves never seen in training. Gross Kt ranges 2.14 to 5.14 on the test set. Fitted kernel `65.6**2 * Matern(length_scale=[19.9, 17.1], nu=2.5)`.

| training solves | test RMSE (Kt) | max abs error | max relative error | test points inside 95% band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 3.50e-03 | 9.01e-03 | 0.306% | 98% |
| 20 | 1.62e-03 | 5.97e-03 | 0.278% | 100% |
| 40 | 4.93e-04 | 2.59e-03 | 0.076% | 100% |
| 60 | 1.08e-04 | 4.12e-04 | 0.019% | 100% |

A full solve takes 0.77 s on average, the surrogate 8.7 microseconds per point, a speed-up of about 88,790 times. Refitting from four different starting length scales changes test RMSE by at most 1.1e-08.

Caveat. Kt is a smooth function of two inputs, so this is an easy problem for a Gaussian process. The result shows the workflow (verified solver, design of experiments, held-out test, calibrated uncertainty), not a research contribution.
